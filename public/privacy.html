Privacy Policy

Lokizenith AI · Lokiz Studios · Effective November 19, 2026

What we store

When you create an account we store your username, a password hash, optional Google profile details, plan and usage counters, and a session token so you stay signed in. Chats, LokiCode files, settings, profile fields, and Team Chat messages you send are stored so they can sync across your devices.

Device and session data

Sign-in records a basic device description (browser, operating system, language, and IP address) so we can show security information and support “log out other devices.” Signing out other devices deletes those session tokens. It does not delete your chats.

Team features

Messages, reactions, pins, threads, and activity in a Team Chat room are visible to members of that room. Voice rooms send audio directly between participants using WebRTC. Our server relays only the signaling needed to connect the call, not a recording of the call. Profile cards show the name, badges, and stats you choose to display.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by PayPal. We store the subscription id and plan status PayPal returns. We do not store your full card number.

AI providers

When you send a prompt, the text (and attachments you include) is sent to the model provider that answers that request so a reply can be generated. Do not submit secrets you would not want processed that way.

Your choices

Settings → Data & Privacy lets you export a JSON copy of your chats, settings, and profile, and sign out other devices. You can also clear data stored in this browser from Settings → Data. Logging out removes the session token from that browser.

How long we keep it

Account data stays until you delete it or we close the account. Server logs used for security are kept only as long as needed to operate and protect the service.
